\chapter*{Abstract\markboth{Abstract}{}}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Urban bus operators increasingly have to plan conventional and electric buses in
one common system.  This creates an integrated planning problem: candidate trips
must be selected, trips must be chained into vehicle blocks, vehicle types must
be assigned, and electric buses must be charged without violating terminal
capacities.  This thesis studies this problem for the MINOA Senior benchmark.
The goal is to produce high-quality feasible schedules for mixed ICE/EV fleets
under headway rules, vehicle-block compatibility, battery autonomy, charging
requirements, parking and charger capacities, and the official
vehicle-scheduling cost structure.

I develop a multi-start path-cover matheuristic for this setting.  For each
timetable variant, the method builds a compatibility graph in which selected
trips are nodes and feasible vehicle continuations are directed edges.  Vehicle
blocking is then modeled as a path-cover problem.  The final method combines
graph matching, charging-aware edge scoring, EV/ICE block assignment, battery
simulation, charging insertion, and a no-regression archive.  The matching step
is exact for a fixed graph, while the complete method remains heuristic because
timetable generation, edge scoring, fleet assignment, and charging decisions are
explored through bounded candidate variants.

Four algorithmic variants are implemented and compared: a greedy constructive
heuristic, an unweighted path-cover method, a weighted path-cover method, and
the final multi-start weighted path-cover matheuristic.  The implementation
computes the official objective internally and writes MINOA-compatible output
files.  The experiments were run in a CPU-only local environment without GPU or
HPC resources, which is encouraging from a reproducibility perspective.  Final
outputs are checked by an independent feasibility and objective audit.  The best
validated headline results produced by the implemented method
are 162.44 for Small, 371.35 for Medium, and 1163.35 for Large, using 2, 5,
and 15 vehicles.  For all Senior instances, the final archive reports total
validated cost 10000.48 and 126 vehicles.

\vspace{0.35\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords: }integrated timetabling, vehicle scheduling,
mixed bus fleets, electric buses, graph matching, path cover, charging
coordination, no-regression archive, MINOA Senior benchmark.
